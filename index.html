🧮 Mission 3: Money Maths

Price = how much something costs.
Total = the prices added together.
Change = the money you get back.

Example: A sandwich costs $6. You pay $10. Your change is $4.

All prices are imaginary classroom dollars ($).

🚀 Mission 4: Be Going To

We use be going to for future plans and intentions.

✅ Affirmative

I am / He is / They are + going to + base verb

I am going to buy a sandwich.
She is going to save money.
We are going to play after school.

❌ Negative

am not / isn't / aren't + going to + base verb

I am not going to waste money.
He isn't going to buy candy.
They aren't going to spend $20.

❓ Questions

Am / Is / Are + subject + going to + base verb?

Are you going to buy lunch?
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is she going to save money?
Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
